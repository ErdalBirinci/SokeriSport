Problem

Same glucose, two completely different outcomes.

The rule books say "carbs before exercise", but which sport, which duration, which trend? Any plan that ignores all three variables is half a plan on the field.

High-intensity intervals

Basketball · Indoor soccer

In the first 10–20 minutes adrenaline lifts glucose; that "I'm fine" feeling is misleading. In the second half muscle glycogen empties and insulin sensitivity rises: the sharp drop usually arrives at the end of the session.

  • CHO oxidation42–46 g/h
  • Sensitivity increase×1.45
  • Risk windowmin 60–90
Endurance

Cycling · Running

A steady pace lowers glucose linearly. There is no adrenaline bump; with IOB on board the drop starts in the first 30 minutes and keeps going at a constant rate. Without hourly carbs, hypoglycemia by minute 45 is almost certain.

  • CHO oxidation58–60 g/h
  • Sensitivity increase×1.65–1.70
  • Risk windowmin 30–60

Ignore IOB and most of the drop is caused by insulin.

The most overlooked variable

IOB: the invisible dose

2.4 U of active insulin at 45 mg/dL/U sensitivity means a 108 mg/dL drop. With the exercise sensitivity multiplier that becomes 157 mg/dL. Any plan that reads glucose alone ignores this debt.

Evidence

Thresholds from guidelines,
assumptions written in plain sight.

The engine uses the thresholds from Riddell et al.'s consensus statement on exercise management in type 1 diabetes and the EASD/ISPAD CGM position statement. Wherever modelling is involved (muscle draw, IOB distribution) the assumption is labelled explicitly.

  • <70 mg/dLExercise contraindicated — treat hypoglycemia first
  • 90–180 mg/dLPre-exercise target band
  • >250 mg/dLCheck ketones; no exercise if positive
  • 0.5–1.2 g/kgRecovery carbohydrate range

FAQ

Common questions.

Does this app give medical advice?

No. SokeriSport is an education and simulation tool; it does not diagnose or adjust treatment. Work with your diabetes team on insulin and carbohydrate decisions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Where do I find my IOB value?

Your insulin pump, smart pen or diabetes app (e.g. CamAPS, Control-IQ, Loop, mySugr) shows active insulin. If you do not know it, the "I don't know my IOB" option assumes 1.5 U in cautious mode and states that clearly.

Which sports are supported?

Basketball, indoor soccer, football, tennis, cycling, running, swimming, rowing, HIIT, strength training and walking. Each profile carries a different carbohydrate oxidation rate and insulin sensitivity multiplier.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The app runs entirely in your browser; your profile and history stay in your device's local storage. A connection is only needed for the first load, then it works offline.

I don't use a pump — is it still useful?

Yes. The carb plan, trend projection, in-session fueling and late-hypoglycemia warnings work without a pump. The basal scenario only appears for pump users.
